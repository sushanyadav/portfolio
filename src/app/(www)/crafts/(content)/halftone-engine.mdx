A rendering engine that converts images and video into halftone dot patterns in real-time. No shaders — just SVG filters and Canvas 2D.

## Techniques

- **SVG filter chain** — feTurbulence + feComponentTransfer for dot quantization
- **Canvas sampling** — brightness-to-radius mapping per grid cell
- **Blend modes** — multiply and screen compositing for color halftone

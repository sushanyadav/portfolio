A click-triggered video transition that briefly renders the frame as a pencil sketch on white paper before revealing the next clip. Built with React Three Fiber and custom GLSL.

Edge detection turns the video into line art, noise displaces the wipe edge, and a quick chromatic aberration flash punctuates the end. Claude helped me simplify the shader math.
